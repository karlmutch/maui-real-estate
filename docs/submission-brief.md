# Verbal Submission: Sustainable Pathways to Maui Residency

Kia ora, Chair and Members.

I support the County's objective of expanding durable housing opportunities for Maui residents. I ask that Maui Kamaole be considered through that same objective - not as a choice between visitor use and housing, but as an established pathway through which ownership supports long-term Maui residency and housing.

I have submitted written testimony with supporting details.

For many people, purchasing a home and becoming a full-time resident, or taking occupancy of a property do not happen at the same time. Education, military service, employment, family responsibilities, and financial circumstance can delay a return to Maui or occupancy here by many years.

During that transition, lawful short-term rental flexibility can help an owner retain the property. It can help meet mortgage payments, homeowners' association assessments, insurance, maintenance, capital-improvement, and reserve costs. In short it can take something not affordable and make it manageable.  The STVR function should not be understood solely as visitor activity. For some owners, it is the financial bridge that makes long-term ownership possible until full-time occupancy becomes practical.

That resilience also matters beyond an individual owner. Owners who can sustain their properties contribute to a financially healthy condominium community. Stable assessments, adequate reserves, appropriate insurance, and sound governance help preserve access to conventional mortgage financing. That access matters to future purchasers who depend on financing rather than cash, including people seeking to establish, or maintain a lasting connection to Maui.  We should learn from condominium communities where financial stress has challenged access to conventional financing and made long-term ownership less attainable, and avoid policy changes that could weaken the conditions supporting sustainable ownership.

My testimony is straightforward: recognize that Maui Kamaole's established resort model and H3/H4 zoning can support, rather than conflict with, the County's broader housing objectives. Preserve the flexibility that enables sustainable ownership to become long-term residency, while continuing to measure actual outcomes for Maui residents to make informed policy decisions.
